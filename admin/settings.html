Stock Reservation Threshold

When a deal's win probability reaches this percentage, StockIQ will automatically reserve the stock for all line items on that deal — preventing it from being sold to other customers. Reservations are released if the probability drops below this threshold or the deal is lost.

%

Set to 0% to reserve all deals immediately. Set to 100% to never auto-reserve.

Stock Alerts

Get notified when a product goes low or out of stock. Alerts fire automatically after every stock change.

Stock Decrement Trigger Stages

Select which deal stages should automatically reduce stock. When a deal moves to any checked stage, StockIQ will decrement stock for all line items on that deal and convert any reservations to a sale.
